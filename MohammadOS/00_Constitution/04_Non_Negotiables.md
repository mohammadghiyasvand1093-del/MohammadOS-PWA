# 1. Purpose

This document defines the rules that are **never negotiable** inside MohammadOS.

If the Mission defines the destination,
and the Decision Engine defines how decisions are made,
this document defines the behaviors that are never allowed to change.

These rules remain active regardless of mood, stress, failure, pressure, or temporary circumstances.

---

# 2. Core Identity Constraints

```text
I do not act based on mood.
I act based on system rules.
```

```text
I do not abandon long-term goals for short-term relief.
```

```text
I do not restart from zero after failure.
I continue from the last valid state.
```

```text
I do not negotiate with avoidance behavior.
```


The failure is never repeated consecutively.
