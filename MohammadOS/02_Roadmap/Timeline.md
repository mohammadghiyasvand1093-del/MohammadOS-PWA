# Purpose

This document defines the planning horizons of MohammadOS.

It translates the long-term roadmap into actionable timeframes, ensuring that daily execution remains aligned with long-term objectives.

Every goal should belong to one timeline.

---

# Planning Horizons

| Horizon | Duration | Purpose |
|----------|----------|---------|
| Vision | 10+ Years | Long-term life direction |
| Strategic | 3–5 Years | Career phases |
| Annual | 12 Months | Major yearly objectives |
| Quarterly | 3 Months | Milestones and focus areas |
| Monthly | 1 Month | Execution priorities |
| Weekly | 1 Week | Tactical goals |
| Daily | 1 Day | Action and execution |

---

# Long-Term Vision (10+ Years)

Target:

Become a highly skilled Backend → DevOps → DevSecOps engineer with international career opportunities, financial independence, and a sustainable professional life.

---

# Strategic Timeline (3–5 Years)

Objectives:

- Complete university.
- Master Backend Engineering.
- Transition into DevOps.
- Build a strong GitHub portfolio.
- Gain professional engineering experience.
- Prepare for international career opportunities.

---

# Annual Timeline

Each year should define:

- Primary technical focus.
- Major learning Gates.
- Career objectives.
- Financial objectives.
- Personal development goals.

Annual planning is reviewed during the Yearly Review.

---

# Quarterly Timeline

Every quarter should contain:

- Active Gate
- Key projects
- Skill targets
- Review checkpoints

Quarterly plans are managed inside the Dashboard.

---

# Monthly Timeline

Every month should define:

- Learning objectives
- Project progress
- Habit targets
- Financial review
- Health review

Monthly plans should remain achievable and measurable.

---

# Weekly Timeline

Each week should answer:

- What must be completed?
- Which Gate is active?
- Which project moves forward?
- What will be reviewed?

Weekly planning connects strategy to execution.

---

# Daily Timeline

Daily planning focuses on execution.

Each day should include:

- Today's Mission
- Deep Work
- Critical Tasks
- Habit Tracking
- End-of-Day Review

Daily execution is managed through the Dashboard.

---

# Timeline Principles

- Long-term goals determine short-term priorities.
- Daily tasks must support weekly objectives.
- Weekly objectives must support monthly goals.
- Monthly goals must support quarterly milestones.
- Quarterly milestones must support the roadmap.
- Every level should reinforce the one above it.

---

# Review Schedule

| Review | Frequency |
|----------|-----------|
| Daily Review | Every day |
| Weekly Review | Every week |
| Monthly Review | Every month |
| Quarterly Review | Every 3 months |
| Annual Review | Every year |
