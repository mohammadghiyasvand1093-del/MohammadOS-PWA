# AI Council

**MohammadOS — Multi-AI Coordination Layer**

**Module:** AI Council
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Active
**Classification:** Strategic Coordination System

---

# Purpose

The AI Council defines how multiple AI systems are used inside MohammadOS.

It prevents:

* Duplicate reasoning
* Conflicting outputs
* Over-reliance on a single AI
* Decision confusion
* Fragmented context

It ensures AI systems act as a **single coordinated intelligence layer**, not separate tools.

---

# Core Principle

> AI systems are advisors, not authorities.

Final decisions always belong to the system owner.

# AI Usage Rules

## Rule 1 — No Redundancy

Do not ask multiple AIs the same question unless results conflict.

---

## Rule 2 — Role Separation

Each AI must be used only for its designated strength.

---

## Rule 3 — Final Authority Rule

```
AI Output ≠ Decision
AI Output = Input Only
```

---

## Rule 4 — Conflict Resolution

If AI outputs conflict:

1. Identify reasoning differences
2. Prefer system principles (Constitution)
3. Use human judgment for final decision

---

## Rule 5 — Minimal AI Dependency

AI should:

* Accelerate thinking
* Not replace thinking

Overuse of AI = Planning Loop Failure

# Failure Conditions

AI Council fails when:

* Same question asked to multiple AIs without reason
* AI output is treated as final decision
* Role boundaries are ignored
* Conflicting outputs are not resolved

---

AI Council is successful when:

* Each AI is used in its optimal role
* Output quality increases
* Cognitive load decreases
* Decision clarity improves
