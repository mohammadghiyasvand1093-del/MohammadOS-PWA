# Purpose

This document defines the major milestones of MohammadOS.

A milestone represents a real-world achievement that demonstrates measurable progress.

Unlike learning Gates, milestones focus on outcomes rather than study.

---

# Milestone Rules

Every milestone must:

- Have a clear objective.
- Be measurable.
- Produce tangible results.
- Be verifiable.
- Support the long-term roadmap.

Milestones should reflect real progress, not just completed courses.
